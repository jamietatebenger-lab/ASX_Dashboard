# NT mining corporate relationship / litigation trace

**Evidence date:** 25 October 2026. **Projects remain separate:** Molyhil (ML23825), Harts Range garnet (ML23868), Barrow Creek / Home of Bullion (EL23186), and other NT titles.

## Published judgment: [2009] FCA 1307 (Harts Range)

Primary: https://www.judgments.fedcourt.gov.au/judgments/Judgments/fca/single/2009/2009fca1307

Federal Court of Australia, Foster J, 11 November 2009, **NSD 872 of 2009**:
- **Applicants:** Imperial Granite & Minerals Pty Ltd, ACN 080 314 012; and John William Benger.
- **Respondent:** Olympia Resources Limited, ACN 077 221 722.
- **Cross-respondent:** Alan Kevin Lockett.
- Paragraphs 1–2 describe a **pleaded contract claim** based on an **Option Deed and Amending Deed** and a separate Trade Practices Act claim.
- Orders 1–2 permitted discontinuance of the Trade Practices Act claims; order 4 required the applicants to pay resulting costs thrown away, if not agreed.
- Order 3 permitted discontinuance of Olympia's cross-claim against Mr Lockett on terms of no costs order.
- Order 6 **transferred the remaining proceedings to the Supreme Court of the Northern Territory**; the Federal Court did NOT decide the contract's merits or finding of breach.
- Judgment records applicants' solicitors as **Minter Ellison**, respondent's as **Freehills**. A solicitor's name is a historical document-custody lead, not a claim about their present files.

## Identity safeguards

- **Sunsphere Pty Ltd ACN 112 922 497** became **Molyhil Mining Pty Ltd** 2 April 2007 according to [ABR](https://abr.business.gov.au/AbnHistory/View?id=47112922497).
- A *different* Sunsphere Pty Ltd, **ACN 670 354 435**, appears in [ABR](https://abr.business.gov.au/ABN/View?id=62670354435). Never merge companies on name alone.
- Shared address, directorship overlap, litigation mention, alleged breach, or a company shareholding does **not** prove hidden beneficial ownership, illegality, liability, a personal receipt, or a current royalty.
- ASIC current-and-historical extracts and roles-and-relationships extracts are the next primary records for officeholder overlap and company identity.
